## Force law and scope
For constant spherical diameter, m=rho_l*pi*d^3/6, A=pi*d^2/4, w=u_g(r_d)-v_d and Re_p=rho_g*d*|w|/mu_g. Acceleration is F(Re_p)*w/tau_p+(1-rho_g/rho_l)*g_p, tau_p=rho_l*d^2/(18*mu_g). The existing documented hybrid is preserved: F=1 for Re_p<=0.01, otherwise F=1+0.15 Re_p^0.687, with Re_p<800 enforced at every RK stage. This stable response form equals the spherical C_D drag force without dividing by zero at zero slip. There is no additional jet force, velocity addition or imposed PZT-only deceleration.

Nominal diameter is 10 micrometres, estimated rather than measured. Provenance: User-selected nominal model estimate: 5 um nominal mesh aperture, emerging jet diameter assumed equal to aperture; Rayleigh d_drop approximately 1.89*d_jet = 9.45 um, rounded to 10 um. Not measured sizing or a validated size distribution. Recorded fluid properties and sources are retained (20 C; assumed 101325 Pa; humidity unavailable). Constant diameter and no evaporation are assumptions. Gravity is [0.898861356725992, -9.765369039820497] m/s^2 in the inclined plume frame, not silently reset to a vertical remounting. The independent measured ZNMF mean is a steady, one-way-coupled prescribed gas: phase motion and gas modification by loading are unresolved. These are conditional calculated paths, not measured combined-flow or automatically ensemble-mean trajectories. The PZT-only ensemble profile is a comparison, not a later constraint or force, and does not establish stationary ambient air.
